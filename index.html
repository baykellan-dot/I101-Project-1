Network Link Analysis

Network link analysis is a data-driven process that maps out relationships between different entities. Concerning politics, network link analysis can be used to track the relations, influences, financial activities, and contracts that occur between elected officials, donors, lobbyists, and other affiliates. First, public records such as campaign finances, corporate registries, court documents, and the like are combined into a single network of data. There must be scrutiny when examining entities, as what may appear to be two similarly named entities in the data is actually just the same entity. This data is organized as a graph, that algorithms then search for patterns that a human wouldn't be able to find on their own. However, it is imprtant to note that this computing technique merely finds all relationships that have the potential to be suspicious, and it is up to human operators to discern which results of network link analysis are truly worth investigating.

This process is commonly used among investigative journalists to uncover corruption and expose its participants, while its use by official government oversight bodies remains novel. This process is an excellent way to discover behavior unfitting of elected officials, however it does not definitively reveal all cases of corruption occurring. In fact, the process is only as strong as the data put into it. If the right data isn’t fed to the network, no significant results may be found and justice may not be served. The technique has the potential to change the field in several major ways. If the process became a standard in political investigation, the type of data relevant to it would become much more valuable. While network link analysis has the potential to make certain types of corruption more difficult to get away with, I fear it is more likely to highlight this vulnerability to corrupt politicians and ther associates, allowing them to adapt their methods and be even harder to catch. That is to say, initially the technique could expose a plethora of corrupt individuals but long-term only cause government corruption to be more discrete and sophisticated in order to evade this data technique.

AI-Powered Natural Language Processing

Using artificial intelligence for natural language processing involves feeding it written language for it to analyze, summarize, and make conclusions about. Within politics, this technique has the potential to revolutionize how political campaigns use data about constituents. Prior to this technology, obtaining an understanding of what constituents believe in and what their priorities are was an extremely time-consuming process. Low-level campaign employees and interns used to have to read every email, social media post, etc individually. However, AI can now do this job far quicker and with access to a massive amount of data. Instead of anecdotal observations about the general beliefs of constituents, AI can generate accurate in-depth reports that provide more insightful conclusions for the campaign office.

This computation technique is widely used among candidates, since it provides an extensive amount of information about voters. This data can reveal what issues voters care about and how much, along with other useful insights that are dependent on the voter-base being analyzed. While I think this tool can be useful, I worry that the more candidates know about us, the better they can deceive us.
